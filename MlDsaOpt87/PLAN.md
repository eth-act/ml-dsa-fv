# MlDsaOpt87: proof plan

## Status (v4, round 4)

Target: `MlDsaOpt/87/opt.hex` (v4: 23,798 bytes, 12,313 instructions, memory
2897 words, unchanged from v3; the proved v3 bytes are `MlDsaOpt/87/v3/opt.hex`,
v2 in `MlDsaOpt/87/v2/`). The four final theorems in `MlDsaOpt87/Main.lean`
are stated with `code := MlDsaOpt87.optBytecode` (the v4 bytes) and
`Gas.schedule`. `checks/Axioms.lean` prints their axioms and lists every
declaration with a direct `sorry`. The method and most of the contract changes
are those of ML-DSA-65 (`MlDsaOpt65/PLAN.md`, "Status (v4, round 4)").

**Closed**: no direct `sorry`; the four final theorems depend on `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only.
